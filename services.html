Our Services

Residential

From new builds to major renovations, we look after the complete electrical installation for your home, from the first rough-in and switchboard through to architectural lighting and the final fit-off. We work closely with builders and homeowners so every power point, switch and light is exactly where it's needed, and the finished job is safe, compliant and built to last.

Commercial

We deliver electrical installations for offices, retail spaces and commercial fit-outs, including power distribution, lighting and data cabling. Our team coordinates with builders and other trades to keep projects on schedule, and we organise the work around your business to minimise disruption and downtime.

Smart Homes

We design and install smart lighting, home automation and integrated electrical systems that suit the way you live. From lighting scenes and app-controlled devices to security cameras and network cabling, we combine modern technology with careful installation to create a connected home that's simple to use every day.
