NEW HOMES & RENOVATIONS

Residential

We work on new homes, extensions and major renovations, whether developing the electrical design or working from existing plans. With thoughtful lighting, quality materials and close attention to the finishing details, we help bring the architect’s and builder’s vision together.

  • New-home electrical installations
  • Major renovations & extensions
  • Architectural & feature lighting
  • Switchboard Upgrades & Rewiring
  • Data Cabling & Network Installations
  • EV Charger Installation

Commercial

We deliver electrical installations for offices, retail spaces and commercial fit-outs, including power distribution, lighting and data cabling. Our team coordinates with builders and other trades to keep projects on schedule, and we organise the work around your business to minimise disruption and downtime.

  • Commercial electrical installations and fit-outs
  • Office, retail, showroom and workshop fit-outs
  • Electrical design and planning
  • Commercial lighting and lighting controls
  • Power distribution and electrical infrastructure
  • Switchboard installations and upgrades
  • Data cabling and networking

Smart Homes

We design and install smart lighting, home automation and integrated electrical systems that suit the way you live. From lighting scenes and app-controlled devices to security cameras and network cabling, we combine modern technology with careful installation to create a connected home that's simple to use every day.

  • Smart home system design and installation
  • Lighting automation and intelligent controls
  • System programming and configuration
  • Smart switches, dimmers and lighting scenes
  • KNX, C-Bus and Dynalite system
  • DALI lighting control
  • Shelly, Clipsal Wiser and SAL Pixie systems
