Who we are

BBG Electrical delivers commercial electrical fitouts, switchboard upgrades and ongoing maintenance for businesses that can't afford downtime. From reception fit-outs to full distribution boards, our licensed team plans the work properly and leaves every site clean, tested and compliant.

We work alongside builders, facility managers and business owners across a wide range of commercial environments — offices, workshops, showrooms and retail fitouts — bringing the same attention to detail to every job, big or small.
